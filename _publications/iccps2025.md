abstract: >-
  In this poster, we propose a gray box Natural Language to Temporal Logic
  translation module. It conditions large language models with temporal logic
  examples and provides an intermediate representation along with the formal
  translation of the natural language property. This intermediate representation,
  a simplified abstract syntax tree, encodes relationships between components of
  the requirement. We establish quantifiable criteria for example quality in the
  training and testing sets, including metrics for formality and specification
  length. Finally, we demonstrate how such structural information consistently
  leads to improved accuracy and show the impact of requirement complexity on the
  proposed approach's performance against state-of-the-art LLM-based benchmarks.
